# Card Game Maker privacy policy

_Last updated: October 31, 2026_

Card Game Maker is published by Deck-Of-Jacks. This policy covers the Windows app from the Microsoft Store and the web version.

## Summary

Card Game Maker collects no personal data, and it does not send any data off your computer.

## Details

- **No data collection.** The app has no telemetry, analytics, crash reporting, accounts, or advertising.
- **No network connections.** The app does not connect to the internet or to any server: no sync, no cloud and no update checks. Its libraries, fonts and help pages are bundled with it. A web link opens in your own browser only if you click it.
- **Your projects stay on your computer.** Work in progress autosaves in the app's local storage (IndexedDB, or localStorage as a fallback) on your computer. Projects you save (`.cgm.zip`), and the PDFs, images, spreadsheets and text files you export, go only where you choose to save them.
- **Files you open stay local.** Spreadsheets (Excel, CSV, ODS), images, fonts, rulebook text and projects you open or drop into the app are read on your computer and are never uploaded.
- **Installed fonts.** If you use *Detect installed fonts*, the font list is read on your computer, only to offer those fonts in the app, and is never sent anywhere.
- **Settings stay on your computer.** App options (such as sidebar width and printer offsets) are saved in the app's local storage.
- **Uninstalling.** Uninstalling the Windows app removes its local storage, including the autosaved project. Projects and exports you saved to files are left in place.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted at this same address with a new "Last updated" date.
