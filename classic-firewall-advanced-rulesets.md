# Classic Firewall Advanced Rulesets privacy policy

_Last updated: November 3, 2026_

Classic Firewall Advanced Rulesets is published by Deck-Of-Jacks.

## Summary

Classic Firewall Advanced Rulesets collects no personal data, and it does not send any data off your PC.

## Details

- **No data collection.** The app has no telemetry, analytics, crash reporting service, accounts, or advertising.
- **No network connections.** The app does not connect to the internet or to any server, and it does not check for updates.
- **Firewall data stays local.** The app reads your Windows Firewall rules on your PC. It changes them only when you apply a change and approve the Windows administrator prompt, or when you switch on triggers, which turn the app's own rules on and off.
- **Triggers.** When triggers are on, the app checks connected network names (including the Wi-Fi network name), the running programs and the clock on your PC to decide which of its rule groups are on. This information is not saved or sent.
- **Remote Desktop safety.** Before applying a change, the app backs up your firewall rules on your PC and, unless you confirm the connection still works, rolls the change back automatically after 60 seconds, so a change that drops a Remote Desktop session is undone. This runs entirely on your PC.
- **Local files.** Your rule groups, firewall backups, a rollback log and a crash log are saved on your PC (in the app's data folder and in Documents\FAR backups). Nothing is uploaded. Uninstalling the app removes the app's data folder; backups in Documents stay until you delete them.
- **Imports.** If you import a Classic Network Monitor export, the app reads only the file you chose (or the newest export in Documents\Classic Network Monitor when running as administrator).
