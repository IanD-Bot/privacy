# Chronos Scroll privacy policy

Chronos Scroll works entirely on your computer.

- **Your notes stay on your device.** They are plain Markdown files and images in `Documents\Chronos Scroll` (or the folder you pick in Settings). The search index, bookmarks and OCR cache sit next to them in a `.chronolog` subfolder.
- **No network access.** The app doesn't connect to the internet: no accounts, no sync, no cloud, no ads, no update checks.
- **No telemetry.** No usage data, crash reports or analytics are collected or sent anywhere. Errors are written only to a local log file in the app's data folder.
- **OCR is local.** Text in pasted images is read with Windows' built-in, offline Windows.Media.Ocr engine. That text only feeds the local search index and is never sent anywhere.
- **Settings** (theme, notes folder, dictionary words) are stored in a local settings file.
- Uninstalling the app removes its settings and logs. Your notes in Documents are left in place.

We collect no personal data, so there is nothing to share, sell or delete on our side.
