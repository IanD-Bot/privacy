# Chronos Scroll privacy policy

_Last updated: October 31, 2026_

Chronos Scroll is published by Deck-Of-Jacks.

## Summary

Chronos Scroll collects no personal data, and it does not send any data off your PC.

## Details

- **No data collection.** The app has no telemetry, analytics, crash reporting, accounts, or advertising. Errors are written only to a local log file in the app's data folder.
- **No network connections.** The app does not connect to the internet or to any server: no sync, no cloud and no update checks.
- **Your notes stay on your PC.** They are plain Markdown files and images in `Documents\Chronos Scroll` (or the folder you pick in Settings). The search index, bookmarks, tasks and text-recognition cache are stored next to them in a `.chronolog` subfolder.
- **Text in images is read on your PC.** Text in pasted images is read with Windows' built-in, offline text recognition (Windows.Media.Ocr). That text only feeds the local search index and is never sent anywhere.
- **Tasks are found on your PC.** Any to-dos found in your notes are picked out with simple local rules, not AI, and are never sent anywhere.
- **Settings stay on your PC.** Your theme, icon color, notes folder and dictionary words are saved in a local settings file. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Uninstalling.** Uninstalling the app removes its settings and logs. Your notes in Documents are left in place.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted at this same address with a new "Last updated" date.
