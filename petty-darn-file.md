# Petty Darn File privacy policy

_Last updated: November 9, 2026_

Petty Darn File is published by Deck-Of-Jacks.

## Summary

Petty Darn File collects no personal data, and it does not send any data off your PC.

## Details

- **No data collection.** The app has no telemetry, analytics, crash reporting, accounts, or advertising.
- **No network calls.** The app does not connect to the internet or to any server. There are no uploads, no sync, no cloud, and no update checks.
- **Files stay where you put them.** A file is opened and saved only in the place you choose. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Text recognition runs on your PC.** OCR is included and runs locally. Recognized text stays on your computer and is never sent anywhere.
- **Signatures and settings stay on your PC.** Saved signatures and settings are stored only on your computer. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Uninstalling.** Uninstalling removes the app. Files you already saved are left where you put them.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted at this same address with a new "Last updated" date.
