# The U in Web privacy policy

_Last updated: November 2, 2026_

The U in Web is a single web page, published by IanD-Bot at https://iand-bot.github.io/The-U-in-Web/, that shows you what your browser reveals to the websites you visit.

## Summary

The U in Web collects and stores no personal data. Everything it shows is worked out inside your browser. The only time it contacts another service is when you click "Show my IP".

## Details

- **No data collection.** The page has no analytics, telemetry, accounts, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
- **No cookies or storage.** The page sets no cookies and writes nothing to local storage, session storage or IndexedDB. Its checks only read whether those features are turned on.
- **Worked out in your browser.** Device and browser details, the fingerprint ID, the ad-blocker and cookie checks and the WebRTC check are computed on your device. They are never sent anywhere and are gone when you reload or close the page.
- **IP lookup only when you click.** "Show my IP" sends one request from your browser to ipwho.is, or to ipapi.co if that fails. That service receives your IP address and standard browser details in order to answer, under its own privacy policy. Nothing is looked up unless you click.
- **Permission demos.** Location, camera and microphone device names, notifications and clipboard are requested only when you click their buttons. Results are shown on the page and then discarded. Your browser may remember that you allowed or blocked a permission; you can reset that in your browser's site settings.
- **Hosting.** The page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub keeps standard server access logs, such as IP addresses, for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.
- **Outside links.** Links to other sites (such as a DNS leak test or similar tools) load only if you click them, and those sites have their own policies.
