# Classic Music Maker privacy policy

_Last updated: November 25, 2026_

Classic Music Maker is published by Deck-Of-Jacks.

## Summary

Classic Music Maker collects no personal data, and it does not send any audio, projects, or usage data off your PC.

## Details

- **No data collection.** The app has no telemetry, analytics, crash reporting service, accounts, or advertising.
- **No network connections of its own.** Classic Music Maker runs offline on your PC and does not upload songs, recordings, or device lists, and does not check for updates.
- **Microphone and audio interfaces.** You can choose a microphone or USB audio interface to record vocal or instrument layers. The app checks available device names when you open the input picker, and only starts capturing audio from your microphone when you explicitly press **Record**.
- **Projects, recordings, and exports stay on your PC.** `.classicmusic` project bundles (which keep your notes, mix settings, and recorded audio together) and exported WAV, MP3, M4A, or MIDI files are written only to the folders you choose on your PC.
- **Settings.** Your theme preference (Match Windows, Light, or Dark) is saved locally in your Windows user app-data folder.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted at this same address with a new "Last updated" date.
