# Classic Devices and Printers privacy policy

_Last updated: November 10, 2026_

Classic Devices and Printers is published by Deck-Of-Jacks.

## Summary

Classic Devices and Printers collects no personal data, and it does not send any data off your PC.

## Details

- **No data collection.** The app has no telemetry, analytics, crash reporting, accounts, or advertising.
- **No network connections.** The app does not connect to the internet or to any server, and it does not check for updates. It does not scan the network or hunt for unpaired devices on its own.
- **Devices and printers on this PC.** The list shows printers, scanners, and Bluetooth devices that Windows already knows about. Status, the default printer, battery level (only when Windows exposes one), and similar details are read from Windows on your PC. The app does not invent device information.
- **Actions you choose.** Add a printer, Add a device, print-queue, test-page, properties, preferences, connect, disconnect, rename, remove, and similar buttons open built-in Windows wizards or dialogs, or ask Windows to change a device you selected. Those Windows components have their own behavior. A short-lived helper runs only when a local printer rename or removal needs administrator permission, and only after you approve the Windows prompt. Cancelling that prompt leaves everything unchanged.
- **Settings stay on your PC.** Your preferences, including the option to hide virtual printers and the last window size and position, are saved in a settings folder in your Windows user profile. Nothing is uploaded. Uninstalling the packaged app removes its data.
